📈 Sales Performance Reporting & Dashboard Automation

**Role:** Data Analyst End-to-End Reporting & Analytics 
**Industry:** Internet Service Provider / Telecommunications
**Tools Used:** Excel, MySQL, Power BI, DAX, Looker Studio

**Note:**: This case study is based on an anonymized and simulated version of a real-world sales reporting workflow. Company names, figures, and identifying details have been modified to protect confidentiality.

### 📌 SITUATION
During its initial phase, NETCOM expanded rapidly by opening new branches across Indonesia. However, this growth lack of centralized reporting system. Management was overwhelemed with a massive volume data transactions from all over regions which remained scattered, uncleaned, and stored in raw formats. Furthermore, executive team lacked of clarity on which metrics should be tracked. It making highly difficult to monitor the achivemnet, trend, and growth of business.

### 📌 TASK
As a role Data Analys which directly recruited by management, i was challenged to design and build the centralized reporting system from scratch. 
My responsibilities included : 
1. Consolidating transaction data from all regionals branches into a centralized valid information source system.
2. Indetifying and defining a key metrics of main business that aligned with executive decision making.
3. Modernizing the reporting workflow as growing data volumes exceeded conventional spreadsheet.

### 📌 ACTION
To achieve these objectives, I executed a strcutured data transformation strategy into three phases ;

**Phase 1: Standarization and Assesement workflow (Excel)**
Compiled manual data from all regional branches and built a template using Excel formulas automatically in order to streamline a day to day, weekly, and monthly report quickly.

**Phase 2: Skalabilitas & Pembersihan Data (SQL) Scalability and Cleaning data (MySQL)**
As data volume and regionals scaled up, I migrated the workflow using SQL. Wrote queries to mine and clean data intensively. Including eliminating data anomalies (such as 'Unknown/Blank' variables in the Supervisor dataset).

**Phase 3: Desain Visual & Penyampaian Cerita Dinamis (Power BI) Visualization and Dinamic Data Story Telling (Power BI)**
1. Designing interactive executive dashboard
2. Developed business metrics by using DAX, like Active Orders Conversation Rate vs Paid Orders, and Average Revenue per Paid Order.
3. Dinamic slicers integrated (Month and Region) to allow executives to perform a real time data exploration.
4. Applied Top-N filter to perform regionals with high performing
5. Enhanced user experience by ensuring the dashboard is fully interactive.

### 📌 RESULT
The executive dashboard designed from scratch was successfully delivered, providing immediate impact on NETCOM’s business performance transparency:

**1. Single Source of Truth:** 
Management can now monitor a total of **21,560 orders** with a total operational revenue of **IDR 3.38 Billion** in a single, real-time, consolidated page.

**2. Strategic Regional Insights:** 
Successfully mapped that while the **Head Office** region dominates transaction volume at **65.55%**, **Bali** emerges as the most lucrative market with the highest transaction quality (**IDR 190K per paid order**).

**3. Operational Efficiency Monitoring:** 
Provided actionable conversion rate visualizations proving that the company's payment conversion remains stable at **~50%** across all branches.

**4. Accelerated Team Evaluation:** 
Allowed management to objectively evaluate field team performance through a clean Supervisor Performance Leaderboard, identifying Arifin as the top-performing Supervisor with 1,710 active orders.
